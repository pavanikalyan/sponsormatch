About the platform

Purpose, privacy & limitations

A transparent student-built approach to community sponsorship.

🔎

Explainable matching

Scores are based on location, interests and budget. They are illustrative, not predictions or trained AI results.

🔒

Security by design

Connected accounts use Supabase authentication and database row-level security. Demo roles are simulated.

🧩

University prototype

No payment processing or real funding transfers. Organisation checks are an administrator review workflow, not legal identity certification.

Data controls

Only demo data is stored locally; connected account data is stored in the configured database.

In Demo Mode you can export your browser-local sample records or reset them to the original examples. In Connected Mode, signing out does not delete your account or database records.

Open original V3

Do not use the prototype for confidential information. Before collecting real data, prepare privacy notices, a retention schedule, safeguarding and operational incident-response processes.
